Sky Dye Privacy Policy

Effective October 2, 2026

Sky Dye collects no data. It doesn't ask for your name, email or an account, it has no ads or analytics, and it has no server of its own. Leaderboards and purchases go through Apple and Google.

Sky Dye is made by Ethan Gottlieb ("I" and "me" below). This policy covers the Sky Dye app for iPhone, iPad and Android, version 3.1 and later.

Game Center and Google Play Games

If you're signed in to Game Center (Apple) or Google Play Games (Google), the game reports your best Survival scores and how far you've climbed in Story to their leaderboards. Apple and Google handle that under their own privacy policies: Apple, Google.

Purchases

Coins are bought through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handles the payment; the game only receives confirmation of what you bought, and never sees your payment details.

On your device

Your progress, coins, unlocks and settings are saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Contact Support

Contact Support in Settings opens an email to me with the app's name and version. What you write, and your email address, reach me only if you send it, and I use them only to reply.

Children

Sky Dye is suitable for all ages and doesn't collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If the game starts collecting anything, I'll update this page and its effective date first.
