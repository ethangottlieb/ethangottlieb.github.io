RoYgBv Privacy Policy

Updated November 20, 2026 for the 4.5 release

RoYgBv has no ads and you can play without an account. The game sends Daily scores to its leaderboard with a device ID so you can see your rank. In the upcoming 4.5 release, gameplay analytics are on by default. You can turn them off in Settings.

RoYgBv is made by Ethan Gottlieb ("I" and "me" below). This policy covers the RoYgBv app for iPhone, iPad and Android, version 4.1 and later.

Gameplay analytics

Starting with version 4.5 (build 34 on iPhone and iPad, and the corresponding Android release), the game starts sending activity to Unity Analytics if you have no saved analytics preference. If you previously chose to turn analytics off, your choice carries over when you update. Version 4.4 does not use Unity gameplay analytics. In the earlier 4.5 TestFlight build 33, analytics were off until you chose to allow them.

To turn analytics off and request deletion, open Settings → Gameplay analytics from the main menu and choose Turn analytics off. This stops gameplay analytics collection and requests that Unity delete the analytics data associated with this installation. You can still play all the same game modes. The next section explains how to delete scores from the separate Daily leaderboard.

I use analytics to see where players get stuck in the tutorial, which modes and puzzles they start or finish, when they use hints, and how often they come back. The game reports the mode, milestone, stage number and board size. These events exclude the contents of shared puzzles and the device ID used for the Daily leaderboard.

Unity processes the events with installation and session identifiers, app version, device information, and country inferred from the internet connection. When analytics are on, Unity In-App Purchasing can also report the product, price and currency, transaction ID, store receipt information and unsuccessful purchase attempts. Apple or Google handles payment. The game does not receive your payment card details. See Unity’s Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

The Daily leaderboard

When you finish a Daily challenge or a Daily Speed, the game sends this to the Daily leaderboard, a server I run on Amazon Web Services:

  • an ID for your device: on iPhone and iPad, Apple's identifier for apps from the same developer on that device; on Android, the device's Android ID for this app;
  • which day's board you played, how many moves you took and how long you took;
  • the name "Unnamed", the same for everyone.

I use this only to show the leaderboard: each board's best scores, and your own best and rank. Everyone who plays sees the best scores, with no names. The ID isn't linked to your name, email or any account, and I don't sell it, share it or use it for anything else. Scores are kept as part of the leaderboard. To have yours deleted, email me.

Game Center and Google Play Games

If you're signed in to Game Center (Apple) or Google Play Games (Google), the game reports how many levels you've completed, and your fastest Speed time for each board size, to their leaderboards. Apple and Google handle that under their own privacy policies: Apple, Google.

Purchases

RoYgBv Pro and hints are bought through the App Store or Google Play. The game uses the store receipt to deliver your purchase. The gameplay analytics section above describes the purchase activity Unity can receive.

Ratings

The game may show the App Store's or Google Play's own rating dialog. Your rating or review goes to Apple or Google, not to the game.

Daily streak reminder

If you allow notifications, the game can remind you on the evening your Daily streak would end. The reminder is scheduled on your device. No server is involved, and nothing about it leaves your device. You can turn it off in the game's Settings or in your device's settings.

On your device

Your progress, settings, purchases and hints are saved on your device, not on my server. Deleting the app deletes them, apart from purchases, which you can restore from the store.

Shared puzzles

Puzzles you share from the level editor are links to a web page hosted on GitHub Pages. The puzzle is written into the link itself, and the page doesn't send it anywhere. GitHub, which hosts the page, may keep standard web server logs.

Contact Support

Contact Support in Settings opens an email to me with the app's name and version. What you write, and your email address, reach me only if you send it, and I use them only to reply.

Children

RoYgBv is suitable for all ages. It doesn't ask anyone for personal information. The Daily leaderboard uses the device ID described above. Gameplay analytics in version 4.5 uses separate identifiers. You can turn it off in Settings as described above.

Changes

If the game starts collecting anything else, I'll update this page and its effective date first.
