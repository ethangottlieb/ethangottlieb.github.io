RoYgBv

Mix colors to make the whole board one color. Red plus yellow makes orange, but red plus orange takes the red back out.

  • 500 handcrafted levels, plus a new puzzle every day
  • Daily Speed: race everyone on the same boards
  • Color symbols for color-blind players

Sky Dye

Mix paint, match the clouds and fly your balloon all the way to the Moon.

  • Story mode through the layers of the Colorsphere
  • Shields, magnets and slow-mo to get out of tight spots
  • Survival mode: how high can you fly?

Side Slide

Bend gravity to touch every side of the ring, in as few switches as you can.

  • 41 handmade levels in four themed packs
  • Lava, bouncy, boost and sticky walls that come alive
  • No ads, no purchases, no data collected
