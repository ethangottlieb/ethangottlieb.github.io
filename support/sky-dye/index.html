Sky Dye Support

Questions, bugs or ideas: I read every email.

Email ethaniragottlieb@gmail.com, or use Settings → Help & About → Contact Support in the game, which starts an email with the game's name and version filled in.

How to play

Fly your balloon up through the clouds. Swipe left or right to drift and tap to stop. Swipe up in the left, middle or right third of the screen to add red, yellow or blue paint, and swipe down to remove it. Paint mixes into the balloon's color: fly through clouds that match it, and dodge the rest.

Prefer pointing? Turn on Tap to steer in Settings and tap where you want the balloon to go.

Telling colors apart

Turn on Color symbols in Settings. Each color gets a symbol made of circles, triangles and squares (one for each drop of red, yellow and blue), shown on the clouds, the paint drops and your balloon.

Coins and purchases

Coins buy a power-up before a level (one coin each). Packs of coins are bought through the App Store or Google Play.

If a purchase was charged but the coins didn't arrive, open the game again while you're online: it finishes any purchase that was interrupted. If they still don't appear, email me with the date of the purchase.

Progress and new phones

Your progress, coins and settings are saved on your device. They aren't backed up to an account, so deleting the app or moving to a new phone starts the game fresh.

Leaderboards

Sign in to Game Center (iPhone and iPad: the Settings app → Game Center) or Google Play Games (Android) to post your Survival scores and Story progress, then tap the award button on the main menu.

Sound and music

Sound, Music and Vibration each have their own switch in Settings. On iPhone and iPad the music stays quiet while other audio, like a podcast, is playing.
