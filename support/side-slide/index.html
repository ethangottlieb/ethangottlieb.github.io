Side Slide Support

Questions, bugs or ideas: I read every email.

Email ethaniragottlieb@gmail.com, or use Settings → Contact Support in the game, which starts an email with the game's name and version filled in.

How to play

Hold anywhere to see where gravity will pull the ball, and let go to switch it. Touch every side that isn't red, and every orb, to win. Red sides end the run; green sides add a ball. Fewer switches earn a better medal.

Aiming

By default you aim from the center of the screen. The aim-mode button (from Level 4) switches to aiming from the ball instead.

Telling colors apart

Turn on Color-blind markers in Settings to mark red and green sides.

Progress and new phones

Your progress, ball skins and settings are saved on your device, not in an account, so deleting the app or moving to a new phone starts fresh. There's nothing to buy.

Leaderboards

Sign in to Game Center (iPhone and iPad) or Google Play Games (Android) to post how many levels you've completed, and how many with gold.
