RoYgBv Support

Questions, bugs or ideas: I read every email.

Email ethaniragottlieb@gmail.com, or use Settings → Help & About → Contact Support in the game, which starts an email with the game's name and version filled in.

How to play

Mix red, yellow and blue on the board's cells until every cell is the same color, in as few moves as you can. Replay Tutorial in Settings → Help & About shows the basics again.

Purchases

RoYgBv Pro and hints are bought through the App Store or Google Play. On a new phone, or after reinstalling, use Settings → Help & About → Restore Purchases to get RoYgBv Pro back. If something you paid for doesn't appear, email me with the date of the purchase.

The Daily challenge

A new board every day, with its own leaderboard. If you allow notifications, the game can remind you the evening your streak would end; turn the reminder off in the game's Settings or your device's settings.

To have your Daily scores deleted, email me.

Progress and new phones

Your progress, settings and hints are saved on your device, not in an account, so deleting the app or moving to a new phone starts fresh (apart from purchases, which you can restore).

Leaderboards

Sign in to Game Center (iPhone and iPad) or Google Play Games (Android) to post how many levels you've completed.
